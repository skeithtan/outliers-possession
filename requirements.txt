# engine was developed and scored with (Python 3.9.0).
#

# it evaluates both learned models (the network and the per-frame model) in numpy, from the two

#
# Only retraining the learned models needs more: torch for the network and scikit-learn for the
# per-frame model (tools/learned_spike/). Neither is listed here, because the engine and the
# evaluation do not need them. The training environments used torch 2.8.0 and scikit-learn 1.6.1.
#
# Optional tooling -- the kloppy ATD loader and anything that opens the video -- lives in
# requirements-starter.txt. Tests need pytest (requirements-dev.txt).
